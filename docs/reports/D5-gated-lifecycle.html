The registered prediction

Registered 12 Oct (docs/research/trial-two/predictions.md): the spread between lanes grows with feature size. On escaped-issue count it did not: four, four, four. What grew apart was kind. Two lanes shipped a feature that forgets everything on reload and passed every automated check; one lane did not. What this does not show

  • Three builders, one per lane, one feature each, four-hour builds on student plans. A method effect and a builder effect cannot be separated.
  • Lane C's builder wrote the framework he built with. Lane A's builder removed the instruction files before starting, as the method requires.
  • Stages 1 to 4 and 8.2 to 8.6 are on the map with citations and were never run.
  • No NBN developer was interviewed. Two practitioners, one workshop record, one supervisor's reviews.
  • The keyboard check on all three previews is partial: the review tooling cannot drive native date pickers. A two-minute human run per lane closes it.
  • On Peepachuu/nbn-sdlc-demo itself the ruleset requires one CI check, not six. The six-check gate was proved on the trial two repositories.
  • The prediction was not supported on count. The paper says so.
