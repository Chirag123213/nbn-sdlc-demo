### 3.4 What this does not show

I report one feature run, with mocked and live evidence answering different questions. There was no pre-feature frontend mutation baseline, and the separate-context agent review did not establish blind seeded-defect discovery or independent human review. The exact original deployment and submitted length were not recovered. These results support neither a productivity-uplift claim nor a cost per gate (claims 6, 14 and 17).

### 3.5 What the manual takes from it

- I would verify worktree configuration before handoff and record approvals, scope exceptions and tool details when they occur.
- I would give reviewers an AC matrix, retain comparable mutation results, and distinguish agent review from human acceptance.
- I would record actual field, request and stored lengths when testing boundaries, plus the deployed commit and action response.
- I would notify users when the textbox limit prevents further input. A pop-up could say: **“Description limit reached: 1000 characters. Additional text may not have been included. Please review before submitting.”** This remains proposed usability work, alongside preserving text after validation errors (claims 8 and 18).
